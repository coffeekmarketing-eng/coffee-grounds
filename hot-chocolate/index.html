02

Make it beautifully

Smooth, glossy and ready in minutes.

The best preparation depends on the product and equipment. This simple café method is a practical starting point. Always confirm the final recipe against the pack instructions before publication.

  1. 01

    Measure

    Start with 25g of powder for a standard 200ml serve.

  2. 02

    Make a paste

    Mix with a small amount of hot milk until completely smooth.

  3. 03

    Add milk

    Pour in the remaining steamed dairy or plant milk and stir well.

  4. 04

    Finish

    Serve as it is, or add a simple topping that complements the chocolate.

White and Thick may require different ratios. Final preparation quantities should be confirmed through Coffee King product testing.
