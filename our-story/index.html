01

The beginning

Every great
cup of coffee
has a story.

I founded Coffee King in 2016 after one too many disappointing cups of coffee.

All I wanted was a great cup of coffee at a decent price, but finding one seemed harder than it should have been.

So, instead of settling, I decided to do something about it. To me, coffee is more than just a drink, it's an experience. From carefully sourcing the finest beans to expertly roasting each batch, every step reflects my commitment to quality, consistency, and craftsmanship. I believe every cup should be a moment to savour, a ritual to enjoy, and a story worth sharing.

Thank you for being part of the Coffee King journey.

Sean Le Tissier

CEO

03

Sourcing

Carefully sourced.
Expertly crafted.

Every Coffee King blend begins with carefully selected beans from trusted coffee-growing regions. We look for coffees with the right balance of flavour, aroma and consistency, then roast and blend them to deliver the smooth, dependable cup people expect from Coffee King.

Selected for flavour. | Roasted for consistency. | Served with care.

04

Responsible growth

Care beyond
the cup.

For us, responsible growth means making better everyday choices across the way we source, serve and grow. That includes reducing waste, improving energy efficiency and giving used coffee grounds a second life through Grounds for Growth.

It also means looking after people, from building a fair and inclusive workplace to supporting communities, charities and the Armed Forces family as Coffee King grows.

Better choices for our coffee, our people and the communities around us.
